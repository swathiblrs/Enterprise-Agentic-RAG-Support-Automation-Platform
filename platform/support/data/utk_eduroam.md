# UT Knoxville eduroam wireless connection

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://utk.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/2277/OIT-Portal/KB/PrintArticle?ID=113604
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, eduroam

Sign into eduroam with your university email address and NetID password. Students use NetID@vols.utk.edu; faculty and staff use NetID@utk.edu. A bare NetID or email alias is not the login described by OIT. If connection fails, forget and re-add eduroam, check network registration, and follow the linked device-specific instructions. Mac users may need updated Keychain credentials. Use the official article for current private-address settings. The ut-open wireless network is unsecured.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
