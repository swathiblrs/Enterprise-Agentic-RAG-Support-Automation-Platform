# UT Knoxville Duo two-factor authentication

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/accounts/2fa/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, duo

Duo two-factor authentication adds another account-verification step beyond a password. Students enroll the day after first registering for classes. Faculty and staff enroll when first signing in. A new phone needs the Duo account restored before sign-in can work. Follow the official OIT two-factor page for enrollment and device-restoration instructions. Contact OIT if you cannot complete verification; this assistant cannot issue bypass codes or change your Duo device.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
