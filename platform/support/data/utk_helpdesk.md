# UT Knoxville OIT HelpDesk contact

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/help/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, helpdesk

The Knoxville OIT HelpDesk assists with NetID, Duo, email, MyUTK, Microsoft Office, security, and other IT services. Call 865-974-9900, use the online Help Request linked from the official HelpDesk page, or chat at https://oit.utk.edu/chat/. Walk-in assistance is available in the Commons at Hodges Library. Consult the official page for current hours and closures rather than assuming round-the-clock staffing.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
