# UT Knoxville NetID and password

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/accounts/netid/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, netid

All UT students, faculty, and staff have a NetID for university services. Look up your NetID using the official NetID page. Use the official password-management links for initial setup, password changes, and resets. Password changes may take up to 15 minutes to synchronize. If access still fails, contact the Knoxville OIT HelpDesk at 865-974-9900.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
