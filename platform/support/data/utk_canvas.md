# UT Knoxville Canvas access

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/teachingtools/online/canvas-administration/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, canvas

Canvas is the University of Tennessee, Knoxville learning management system. Faculty, staff, and students with active UTK UPN credentials can access it. Courses listed by the Registrar have corresponding course shells. Access and course administration follow university policies. For a missing course or enrollment issue, consult the official Canvas administration guidance and OIT; this assistant cannot change a course roster, promise a publication date, or alter grades.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
