# UT Knoxville VPN and off-campus access

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/network/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, vpn

UT VPN provides an encrypted connection to campus-network resources from off campus. Examples include remote access to an office computer, T-Storage H: and I: drives, and the OIT public Linux environment. Microsoft 365, Canvas, and Zoom do not require VPN. Follow the VPN setup instructions linked from the official OIT Network page; do not install a client from an unverified source. For service failures contact OIT and describe the affected resource and error.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
