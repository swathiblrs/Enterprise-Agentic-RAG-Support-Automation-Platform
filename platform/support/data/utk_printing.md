# UT Knoxville VolPrint and MyPrint

Institution: University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Source: https://oit.utk.edu/labs/printing/mobile/
Reviewed: 2026-10-22
Tags: utk, university-helpdesk, printing

MyPrint lets laptops, tablets, and phones submit jobs to most VolPrint printers. Sign in at https://myprint.utk.edu with your NetID and password. Upload your document, select the job, set print options, choose a print location and printer, and confirm. For color output, select color in print options. Go to the selected printer to collect the job. This assistant cannot inspect a print balance or refund a charge.

This is a concise project-authored summary of public guidance. Consult the official source for current procedures. This prototype is not an official UT service.
